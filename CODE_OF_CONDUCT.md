# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Gold Rush to be a friendly, harassment-free project for everyone,
regardless of experience level or background.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps build a positive community:

- Being respectful and welcoming in issues, PRs, and discussions.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its players.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insults, personal or political
attacks, publishing others' private information, and other conduct that
would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions) and when representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer,
[@aliammari1](https://github.com/aliammari1), via a GitHub direct contact
or a private security advisory. Reports will be reviewed and handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and
may ban contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate.
